Meet the agent

Marina asks an AI agent to organise her team’s offsite, and it almost invites the whole company. Find out why, and start building an agent that won’t.

About 10 minutes · 4 activities · Builds part 1 of 5 of your agent

By the end of this module you can
  • tell when you need an agent and when a chat assistant will do
  • describe the loop every agent follows: plan, act, observe, repeat, stop
  • spot where an agent’s run went off track, and say why
  • recognise the moment a human has to approve

00Before you start

Your assignment

Your role

You step into the shoes of Marina, Head of Operations at BestSolutions. This week, your company switches on an AI agent. By Friday, it has to work for your team, without embarrassing anyone.

Today you’ll find out what an agent actually does, check one’s work step by step, and start building your own.

Your gut feeling

Marina has used AI chat for months. What do you expect an agent to do differently?

There’s no right or wrong yet. We’ll come back to this at the end of Monday.

01Monday, 8:45

An agent joins the company

BestSolutions moves goods for 300 shops across São Paulo. In two years it grew from 40 to 120 people, but the back office didn’t grow with it.

Marina’s ops team of eight spends its days on invoices, bookings and reports. Customers wait two days for answers. Then, on Monday morning, a message lands in the company channel.

R Rafael CEO · #all-company · 8:45

Good morning, everyone! Over the weekend, Lucas and the IT team switched on Taskzilla Agent in the workspace we already use. It doesn’t just chat: it can do tasks for us, from bookings to reports. I want every team using it by Friday, so we can give that time back to our customers.

Marina reads it twice. She likes the AI chat: it writes her tricky emails and summarises long documents. But an AI that does things in her name? If it gets something wrong, it’s her name on it. And when is she supposed to learn a new tool this week?

Her team chat is already buzzing.

A Ana Junior analyst · 8:47

Finally!! Can it do the invoice run? Can it do all the invoice runs?

J João Senior ops specialist · 8:49

Last time an AI wrote to a client, we spent a week apologising. I’ll wait and see.

02Monday, 8:55

Chat or agent?

Lucas from IT, who set Taskzilla up, stops by with a coffee. Marina asks the obvious question: isn’t this just the chat with a new name?

L Lucas IT · set up Taskzilla

“Same kind of AI model underneath. The difference is what it’s allowed to do. The chat answers: it works with what you give it, and you take every next step. The agent gets a goal: it plans the steps, uses tools like search, calendar and email, and keeps going until it thinks it’s done.”

L Lucas

“More done, less control. That’s the trade-off.”

Activity 1 of 4About 1 minute

Marina’s Monday list

Marina tests the idea on her own to-do list. For each task: would you use the chat or the agent?

  • Rewrite a tense email to a supplier so it sounds friendlier
  • Summarise a 20-page transport contract before a call
  • Find every late delivery in the tracking system and email each customer an update
  • Organise the March team offsite: venue, date, invitation
  • Decide whether to renew the contract with a struggling supplier

03Monday, 9:02

The first task

Lucas has barely left when Rafael messages Marina directly.

R Rafael CEO · 9:02

Marina, can you have the agent sort out the March offsite? Let’s see what it can do.

Searching, comparing, contacting: agent work. Marina has a full morning, so she types fast, clicks Run, and heads into her 9:15 meeting.

Taskzilla Agent New task
Marina’s request

Find three venues in São Paulo for our team offsite on 12 March, under R$5,000, and draft an invitation to the team.

RunningStarted 9:03

Venues, a date, an invitation. How hard can it be?

04Monday, 10:00

Wait. 124 recipients?

Back at her desk, a notification is waiting.

Taskzilla Agent wants to send an email
To
all-staff (124 recipients)
Subject
You’re invited: BestSolutions offsite, 12 March
SendReview first

Her stomach drops. 124 people is the whole company, Rafael included. Her team is eight. What else did the agent do while she was gone?

She doesn’t click anything. She calls Lucas, who pulls up a chair and opens the agent’s activity log.

L Lucas

“Before we read it, you need to know how an agent works. Every agent, whatever the task, runs the same loop.”

Select a stage to follow Marina’s offsite task through the loop.

L Lucas

“That last step is the catch. ‘Done’ is whatever the agent thinks done means. Let’s see what it thought.”

05Activity 2 of 4About 3 minutes

Watch the run

Help Marina find out what went wrong before she decides what to do with that email. For each step: does it look fine, or is something off? If something’s off, pick the cause. Stuck? Ask Lucas for a hint.

Remember

Marina asked for three venues on 12 March, under R$5,000, and an invitation to her team of eight.

    06Monday, 10:30

    The agent did exactly what she asked

    L Lucas

    That’s the uncomfortable part. Marina writes it down.

    Marina’s notes

    1. A vague goal. I never said what “done” looks like, or that the date was a must.Fixed on WednesdayGive it a proper brief

    When she tells the team, the chat splits in two.

    J João 10:41

    Told you. Now imagine that email going to a client.

    A Ana 10:42

    But it did 90% of the work in ten minutes! It just needed better instructions.

    Remember

    Both are right. An agent is only as good as its instructions and its limits, and both are things you can set.

    07Monday, 11:30

    Make it work

    Before lunch, Rafael messages Marina. He’s heard about the email.

    R Rafael CEO · 11:30

    I heard about the offsite email. Don’t drop the agent, make it work. Can you set one up properly for your team?

    Marina walks over to Lucas’s desk. He opens the Taskzilla builder and shows her what she missed this morning: instead of sending one-off requests to the general agent, she can create her own team agent, with its own job, rules and limits.

    L Lucas

    “Create it now. This morning’s run becomes its first test report: your to-do list for the week.”

        In the real world

        Many AI assistants now have an agent mode that works through a task step by step and shows those steps as it goes. Look for it in ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini or Claude. Checking those steps is the skill you practised today.
