SME Feedback Framework

How I get Subject Matter Experts to give review feedback that my team can act on.

The problem

When Subject Matter Experts reviewed the content my team and I had drafted, I kept getting comments like these:

  • “Not thorough enough.”
  • “Don’t agree.”
  • [A block of new text meant to replace the passage they had marked]

Any feedback is better than no feedback. But comments like these are hard to work with: they say that something is wrong, not what, why, or what better would look like. Replacement text skips the reasoning entirely, so you can’t tell whether it fixes a real gap or just reflects a preference.

The framework

So I made feedback part of every production kick-off. Alongside the timeline and review rounds, the team and the SMEs agree on one structure: every comment answers three questions.

1 · What
What would you change? Point to the exact passage.
2 · Why
Why would you change it? Explain what the learner is missing.
3 · How
How would you change it? Suggest the fix, with a source if there is one.

Simple, but effective.

The result

Feedback started to look like this:

“This part doesn’t go deep enough on data quality. Learners need more on it to understand, in the module on biases, how those biases come about. See the following link on data quality, especially the second paragraph …”

One comment names the gap (data quality), explains why it matters for the learner (it sets up the module on biases) and points to the source to use. My team could act on it right away, without a follow-up meeting.

Try it yourself

Introduce the three questions to your SMEs at your next kick-off, and let me know how much time it saves you in reviews.
