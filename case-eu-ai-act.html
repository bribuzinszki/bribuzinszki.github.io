EU AI Act Essentials

How the MOOC EU AI Act Essentials for the German non-profit learning platform KI-Campus came to be.

My role
Senior Instructional Designer
Team
1 Graphic Designer and Video Editor, 1 Instructional Designer, 3 AI Regulation Experts, 1 Law Professor, 1 Content Editor (KI-Campus)
Audience
Working professionals without law expertise
Timeline
Oct-Feb 2025
Tools
Figma, Miro, Google Doc, H5P, Moodle LMS, HTML
Result
Over 12000 learners after the first 6 months, above 20% completion rate, 4,5/5 rating

The challenge

This project presented a dual challenge across regulatory, organizational, and resource constraints. First, because the EU AI Act was brand new with several provisions still taking shape during development, we had to exercise extreme diligence with our case examples and regulatory statements. I designed the learning architecture to be highly modular and agile, ensuring the course could be easily updated as guidelines evolved. Second, operating with a lean budget and a tight delivery timeline required close coordination across external partners, including university collaborators and the KI-Campus platform. As the Lead Instructional Designer and Project Manager, I established an efficient asynchronous governance structure to drive alignment and manage stakeholder expectations smoothly, reserving live syncs strictly for key project milestones.

Key decisions

We will re-use and recycle

Operating on a lean budget required being resourceful from day one. We leveraged existing drafts and foundational materials from past learning experiences as a base, while our graphic designer established a modular template system that allowed team members to adapt visuals with minimal overhead. By building heavily within Moodle and utilizing pre-styled H5P templates, we streamlined the LMS implementation process. These efficiencies freed up crucial time and budget to produce the project’s non-negotiable video components, ensuring high production value where it mattered most.

We will build an 8 hour course

Stakeholders initially requested a comprehensive, in-depth course, which inherently meant building for a substantial time commitment. When digging into the root of this requirement, I found it stemmed from external platform requests for a "comprehensive overview" robust enough to help laypeople determine when a specialized legal review was genuinely necessary for their AI products. This strategic rationale made sense, so I designed an extensive curriculum focused on keeping learners engaged and motivated across an eight-hour learning journey.

We will build in Moodle with native html

Because the company was actively restructuring its internal learning infrastructure, we knew the course would eventually need to be migrated to a new LMS environment without losing learner tracking or progress data. To future-proof the project and ensure a seamless data transfer later, we committed to keeping the course architecture as Moodle-native as possible. While this meant accepting a slightly more utilitarian visual aesthetic compared to proprietary authoring tools, it prioritized long-term data integrity and system portability.

Outcome

As one of the first courses on the topic in the German-speaking world, the program was exceptionally well received. It was formally recommended by the University of Tübingen for their students and named the top newcomer course on the KI-Campus platform in December 2025. Beyond driving strong learner engagement, the initiative strengthened client relationships and generated valuable collaborative opportunities. Evaluating skill acquisition in a MOOC environment presents unique challenges; however, completion metrics and quiz performance served as strong indicators of mastery, with learners rarely needing more than two attempts to pass module assessments, confirming that the interactive exercises successfully solidified complex concepts. Additionally, the overall completion rate comfortably exceeded industry standards for open online learning.

What I’d do differently

Looking back with the skills and tools I have today, I would leverage custom code components to elevate the visual experience and build even richer, dynamic interactions. However, given the project’s original scope, resource constraints, and timeline, the strategic decisions we made held up exceptionally well, and I remain genuinely proud of the final outcome.
