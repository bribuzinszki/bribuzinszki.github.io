Give it a proper brief

Monday’s agent did exactly what Marina wrote, and she didn’t write much. Help her write Ops Agent’s standing instructions, give it the right files, and run Monday’s request again.

About 10 minutes · 3 activities · Builds part 3 of 5 of your agent

By the end of this module you can
  • write instructions that cover the five parts of a brief
  • rewrite an instruction an agent can’t act on into one it can
  • choose the files an agent needs, and only those

J João from his desk

Monday’s request

“Find three venues in São Paulo for our team offsite on 12 March, under R$5,000, and draft an invitation to the team.”

No wonder the agent failed so badly. It did exactly what she wrote, and she didn’t write much: nothing about who “the team” is, nothing about checking that a venue is actually free that day, and nothing about when to stop and ask her.

02Wednesday, 9:15

The new colleague test

L Lucas IT · set up Taskzilla

“The agent only knows what you tell it and what it can see. Before you send anything, ask yourself: could a smart new colleague do this on day one, from my message alone?”

A brief that passes the test has five parts. Some are true for every task, so they belong in the agent’s standing instructions. Others change every time, so they belong in each request.

PartQuestion it answersLives inOps Agent example
GoalWhat, and why?Each requestBook a day for the ops team to plan Q2 and bond
ContextWhat does it need to know?Instructions“The team” = the 8 people on the ops-team list
LimitsWhat must it respect?BothAlways: never email all-staff. This task: 12 March only, under R$5,000, within 30 minutes of the office
Done whenWhen should it stop?Each requestA table of 3 venues free on 12 March, with price and travel time, plus a draft invitation
Check-insWhen must it ask?InstructionsBefore booking, paying or sending anything
Remember

If you can’t check it, the agent can’t act on it. “Be careful” can’t be checked; “confirm the venue is free on 12 March” can.

04Activity 2 of 3About 1 minute

Attach knowledge

Next to Instructions, the builder has a Knowledge part: files the agent can look things up in. The team’s drive offers six. Attach what Ops Agent needs for the offsite, and leave out the rest.

Remember

An agent treats every file it’s given as true, and it reads all of it.

      In the real world

      Most agent builders have the two parts you filled in today: Instructions (sometimes called a system prompt) and Knowledge, where you upload files. You’ll find them in custom GPTs in ChatGPT, Projects in Claude, Gems in Gemini and agents in Microsoft Copilot Studio. People in tech call this work context engineering.
